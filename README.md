# Bold and Brave

Bold and Brave is a single-player browser game. This repository hosts the **Playable Vertical Slice**: the smallest self-contained version of Bold and Brave that delivers its defining player experience at representative quality and anchors later development phases.

The slice presents one offline-style, single-player journey: the player leads the **Band** from frontier preparation, resolves one timed defense **Local Contract**, and ends in the changed settlement with persistent human and material consequences. The defining experience prioritizes directional melee, personal Band leadership, and a visible **Agent** relationship or **Grievance** consequence.

Canonical terms are defined in [CONTEXT.md](CONTEXT.md). The normative requirements are [docs/requirements.md](docs/requirements.md) and the architecture is [docs/architecture.md](docs/architecture.md).
