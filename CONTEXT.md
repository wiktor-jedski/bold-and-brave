# Bold and Brave

The shared language and guidelines for defining the game and its first playable release.

## Language

**Playable Vertical Slice**:
The smallest self-contained version of Bold and Brave that delivers its defining player experience at representative quality and can anchor later development phases.
_Avoid_: Full game, MVP, prototype

**Band**:
The player-led group comprising the player character, any companions, and ordinary troops travelling and fighting together.
_Avoid_: Warband, party, squad, army

**Combatant**:
An individual participating in a battle, including the player character, Companions, Troops, enemy Agents, bandits, and settlement residents. Combatant describes battle participation and does not replace an individual's social identity or Agent fate.

**Command group**:
A Band member or set of Band members that receives a shared order from the player; the slice uses separate Companion and grouped Troop Command groups, which follow the same tactical rules.

**Hold position**:
A command that anchors a Command group to a designated place in a Scene, where its members defend locally around a visible marker until given another order.

**Companion**:
A socially developed Agent who may join the Band and act with some independence.
_Avoid_: Hero unit, follower, named troop

**Agent**:
A named, persistent non-player character capable of relationships, grievances, and independent action.
_Avoid_: NPC, ambient character, quest giver

**Disposition**:
An Agent's broad current stance toward the player character or Band—`Friendly`, `Neutral`, or `Hostile`—expressed through behavior and interaction rather than a raw relationship score.

**Grievance**:
A persistent remembered wrong or debt involving an Agent, represented by a fixed cause, that can explain and influence that Agent's disposition and reactions.

**Agent relationship**:
The combination of an Agent's Disposition toward the player character or Band and that Agent's active Grievances; it belongs to the named Agent rather than to a shared faction or settlement score.

**Agent fate**:
The persistent post-battle condition of a named Agent: `Active`, `Captive`, or `Executed`. Only an Active Agent has a current Disposition.

**Downed**:
Alive but unable to continue fighting; deliberately presented like a dead combatant during battle until post-battle resolution.
_Avoid_: Dead, defeated, unconscious

**Directional Guard**:
A sword or staff defense held in one of four sectors; matching the incoming attack fully negates it and creates a brief attacker-recoil window.

**Shield Block**:
An omnidirectional defensive state that blocks attacks while held, consuming the user's stamina.

**Captive**:
A Downed former enemy retained by the Band through a post-battle Capture decision.
_Avoid_: Prisoner, slave, recruit

**Troop**:
An individually persistent but socially lightweight member of the Band, defined primarily by equipment, health, and proficiency.
_Avoid_: Companion, unit, disposable soldier

**Feat**:
A persistent capability chosen for the player character as a consequence of progression.
_Avoid_: Perk, talent, skill unlock

**Overworld**:
The simplified strategic map through which the Band travels between notable locations.
_Avoid_: Main map, world map

**Scene**:
A separately loaded 3D space representing a settlement, battlefield, camp, or other notable location entered from the Overworld.
_Avoid_: Level, map, instance

**Settlement condition**:
The binary state of the settlement after the Local Contract: `Safe` when the Band succeeds and `Damaged` when the Band is defeated.

**Raid location**:
The Scene where the defense battle occurs: the bridge when the player is ready before the raid begins or waits in the settlement until the deadline, or the settlement center when the player enters from outside after the attack has begun.

**Raid deadline**:
The time threshold after an Accepted Local Contract when the raid begins; it matters only while the contract is active.

**Settlement resident**:
A person present in the settlement Scene who may be armed or unarmed and is not necessarily a named Agent.

**Journal**:
The player-facing record of the Local Contract, Band preparation, resources, deadline, and persistent consequences.

**Local Contract**:
A consequential undertaking offered at a location and resolved by the Band.
_Avoid_: Quest, mission, job

**Local Contract state**:
The stage of the slice's Local Contract: `Available`, `Accepted`, `Resolved`, or `Failed`.

**Renown**:
The accumulated public recognition of the player character and Band.
_Avoid_: Fame, reputation score

**Coin**:
The Band's general-purpose money for recruitment, equipment, and trade.
_Avoid_: Gold, credits, currency points

**Provisions**:
Consumable supplies needed to sustain the Band while travelling.
_Avoid_: Food points, rations resource
