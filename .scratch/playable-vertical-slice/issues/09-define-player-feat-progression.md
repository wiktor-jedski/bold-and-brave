# Define player Feat progression

## Question

Which small set of player-only Feats can be offered after the contract, how are they unlocked by outcome, and how does each create a visible capability without introducing Troop progression or a broad skill tree?


## Answer

Any successful defense unlocks one Feat choice, including a late settlement-center victory that leaves the Settlement condition `Damaged`. A defeat grants no Feat.

After both survivor-fate decisions and the outcome summary, the player chooses one Feat from the same three fixed choices. The chosen Feat is permanent for the slice, activates immediately, and appears in the Journal. Feats improve existing combat actions and add no new control.

- **Rapid Guard** shortens both Directional Guard sector-transition time and Shield Block raise time.
- **Rapid Attack** shortens both committed-attack wind-up and recovery.
- **Rapid Stamina** increases stamina regeneration rate without changing the existing regeneration delay.

Exact numeric values remain implementation tuning.

## Comments

### Resolution — 2026-08-11

The human confirmed victory-only progression, one permanent choice from three fixed Feats, any-victory eligibility, and award timing after survivor decisions. The human selected faster Directional Guard and Shield Block, faster attack wind-up and recovery, and faster stamina regeneration.
