Lanternlight — an ember-lit dusk defense

The bridge Scene at nightfall. Warm lantern pools carve the Band out of the dark; the enemy stays cold steel until the ember telegraph announces the swing. Compare this treatment with the other designer versions before any direction is chosen.

Intended tradeoff: darkness buys strong emotional focus and makes the ember telegraph the loudest signal on screen — but the world outside the lantern pools carries almost no information, so HUD elements must stay inside the light and resident protection needs an explicit readout.

What to inspect

Silhouette
Mid-height, near-real proportions. Rank reads through gear: the Companion's lantern staff, the enemy Agent's cleaver and crimson sash.
Palette
Warm umber dark against cold steel; ember orange reserved for input, commands, and lit windows.
Lighting
Two lantern pools do the composition work; a vignette hides the unplayed edges of the Scene.
Motion language
Slow sine breath, flame flicker, rising embers; the dashed telegraph is the only fast element.
HUD density
Sparse — contract, deadline, stamina, guard sector, settlement chip; nothing else competes with the light.
Downed language
A Downed combatant drops into shadow as an unmarked heap — indistinguishable from dead until the lantern cone reveals identity after the battle.
