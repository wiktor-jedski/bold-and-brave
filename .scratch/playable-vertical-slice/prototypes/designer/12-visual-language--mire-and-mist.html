Mire and Mist — a fog-drowned dawn at the bridge

Cold morning fog over low water. The Band stands tall and reed-thin; the enemy comes hunched and wide through the mist. The interface leaves the Scene and lives in the margin as a field Journal. Compare with the other designer versions before any direction is chosen.

Intended tradeoff: the strongest grounded-frontier mood of the three — oppressive quiet, figures half-lost in fog — but the lowest raw contrast, so readability is carried by shape language (upright versus hunched), bone-white marks, and motion rather than value separation.

What to inspect

Silhouette
Tall, reed-thin uprights for the Band against wide, hunched enemies — allegiance reads from posture before color. Rank by headgear and the Companion's forked standard.
Palette
Desaturated peat, moss, and fog green-gray; bone white reserved for marks, the Hold stake, and the telegraph; one dull crimson rag on the enemy Agent.
Lighting
Fully diffuse dawn — no cast shadows worth reading, only soft ground patches; depth comes from drifting fog bands and overlap.
Motion language
Slow and heavy: grass sway, breath-weight sag, twenty-second fog drift; the condensing telegraph arc is the only quickening beat.
HUD density
Medium, and moved out of the Scene: a field-journal margin carries contract, deadline, Band status, stamina, and settlement condition; only a guard compass stays on the picture.
Downed language
A Downed combatant sinks under a fog patch with an unmarked stake — indistinguishable from dead until the mist parts on the name tag after the battle.
