Human decision needed. This file is one of three throwaway designer treatments of the same bridge-defense Scene. No direction has been selected; nothing here is production UI. Open the sibling prototypes to compare.

What to inspect

Silhouette
Squat, wide bodies with oversized kite shields; rank is banner height — the enemy Agent towers, the Companion carries a pennon, Troops and bandits are plain.
Palette
Parchment and sand ground; heraldic woad (Band) against madder (enemy); saffron reserved for commands and the telegraph.
Lighting
Flat noon: no shading gradient, short hard shadows straight underfoot, a rayed heraldic sun.
Motion language
Pose-to-pose steps: two-frame bob, fluttering banners, a blinking telegraph wedge. Nothing eases; signals read as events, not ambience.
HUD density
Dense ledger: contract seal and sun-arc deadline up top, Band roster and settlement sign on the right, block stamina and guard fan below.
Downed language
Downed drops to a gray heap with the banner furled and unmarked — anonymous until the banner is re-raised with a name plate and wax-seal decisions after the battle.
